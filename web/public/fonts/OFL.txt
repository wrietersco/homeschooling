Bundled Arabic webfonts
=======================

https://openfontlicense.org

1) Indo-Pak Naskh (PRIMARY)
   File:   lateef-400.woff2, lateef-700.woff2
   Family: Lateef
   Source: https://software.sil.org/lateef/  (via @fontsource/lateef)
   Lateef is SIL's Naskh face tuned for the Arabic script as written in South
   Asia (Urdu/Sindhi/Pakistani conventions) — the closest license-clean match to
   the Indo-Pak Mushaf hand. Used as the self-hosted "Indo-Pak Naskh" face.

2) Quran Naskh (FALLBACK)
   File:   scheherazade-new-400.woff2, scheherazade-new-700.woff2
   Family: Scheherazade New
   Source: https://software.sil.org/scheherazade/  (via @fontsource/scheherazade-new)
   A general Arabic Naskh face, kept as the fallback under "Quran Naskh".

Both are wired in web/src/assets/main.css. To swap in a specific licensed
Indo-Pak Mushaf font (e.g. a publisher's Quran face), replace the relevant
.woff2 files and update the @font-face src — keep this notice accurate for
whatever font is bundled.
